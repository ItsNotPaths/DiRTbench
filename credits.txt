raylib's rlgl and stb_vorbis come from the Odin distribution. rlgl remains as
the low-level OpenGL shim; windowing, input and audio are SDL.

Third-party data, checked in:
  assets/d3/schemaDirt3.xml           Ego-Engine-Modding
    The DiRT 3 database layout. src/d3/database.odin reads database.bin
    through it. Taken as-is from
    github.com/EgoEngineModding/Ego-Engine-Modding,
    src/EgoDatabaseEditor/schema/schemaDirt3.xml

assets/d3/moosylvania-materials.pssg is a shader/texture-name template read out
of a DiRT 3 installation. It carries no texture or model payload, only the
metadata a generated PSSG needs to reference the game's own art. It is
Codemasters' data, not ours.
